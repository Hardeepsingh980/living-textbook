# Living Textbook: demo video script

The video runs 2 minutes 47 seconds. The rules say "strictly under 3 minutes", so keep at least 10 seconds of margin when you put your own recordings in. Every line below has a caption. The reference video uses the Gemini voice "Sulafat" for the narrator. You can keep that voice or record your own voice over the same lines.

The video has 12 shots. Each shot has one "wow" moment. The shots marked **Your phone** need a real phone recording on printed paper. The reference video shows a stand-in there (a recording of the app with a simulated camera) and a red note that says what to record.

## Before you record

1. Print `eval/output/stories/living-textbook-story-pages.pdf` in colour (A4). You need page 3 (The Ashes That Made Trees Bloom: the old couple by the cherry tree) and page 4 (the next page of the same story).
2. Print a science page. Page 1 of the science demo pack (`eval/output/demo-pack/living-textbook-demo-pages.pdf`, the heart) works best. The pendulum page from the reference video also works if you have it.
3. Put each page flat on a desk in good light. Avoid glare from a lamp.
4. Turn on the phone's screen recording (Android: Quick Settings, Screen record; iPad: Control Center, Screen Recording). Also film over your shoulder with a second phone if you can. A shot that shows the paper and the phone together is the strongest proof that this is real.
5. Record in landscape for the over-the-shoulder camera and in portrait for the screen recording. Keep each take short. The video needs only 5 to 12 seconds of each.

## The shots

### 1. Cold open: the page becomes a film (0:00 to 0:22)

- **Wow:** a printed picture turns into a moving film on the paper, and the characters speak.
- **We see:** the printed Ashes page on a desk. The phone looks at it. The reader taps "Bring it to life". In about 3 seconds the printed picture gets a red outline, a film window grows out of it and plays on the page.
- **Narrator:** "This is a page from a class seven English book." / "Watch what happens when a phone looks at it."
- **Then the app speaks:** the story's narrator and the Dog-Spirit say the book's own words. Do not talk over them.
- **Your phone (P1, 12 seconds):** over the shoulder, PDF page 3. Tap "Bring it to life", hold still for 3 seconds, then move the phone slowly so that the film is seen to stay on the page. Add 4 seconds of the phone's screen recording as a close-up if you have it.

### 2. Title (0:22 to 0:32)

- **Wow:** the promise in one sentence.
- **We see:** the title card, with the app playing in a drawn phone.
- **Narrator:** "This is Living Textbook." / "A printed page cannot move, or answer a question. With Google AI, now it can."

### 3. A science page becomes a lesson (0:32 to 0:44)

- **Wow:** Gemini builds the lesson live, and "How it was made" shows each step.
- **We see:** a photo of a physics page about a pendulum goes in. "How it was made" lists the steps. The page comes alive. The reference plays this part at 1.6 times the speed and says so on screen; the real wait is about 18 seconds.
- **Narrator:** "Here is a science page about a pendulum." / "Gemini reads every word, number and picture, plans a lesson, and checks every number against the page."

### 4. On the real page (0:44 to 0:49)

- **Wow:** the drawing moves on the paper itself and stays there.
- **Narrator:** "On a phone, the drawing moves on the paper itself."
- **Your phone (P2, 6 seconds):** over the shoulder, the printed science page. Tap "Bring it to life". When the drawing moves, tilt the phone a little so that the drawing is seen to stay on the paper.

### 5. The tutor, and a question (0:49 to 1:12)

- **Wow:** a student interrupts the tutor with their voice, and the tutor answers at once.
- **We see:** the pendulum lesson. The Gemini Live tutor explains a step. The student asks "Wait. What is one oscillation?". The tutor stops and answers.
- **Narrator:** "A Gemini Live tutor explains each step." / (the tutor speaks) / "Stop it any time, and ask." / (the question and the answer)

### 6. What if (1:12 to 1:22)

- **Wow:** a number changes, and the page changes with it.
- **We see:** the string length goes to 1.5 m, and the time period on the page goes from 2.01 s to 2.46 s. The tutor says why: "the time period gets longer because the swing takes more time".
- **Narrator:** "Change a number, and the page changes too."

### 7. A story page becomes a film you play (1:22 to 1:42)

- **Wow:** the reader decides what happens next.
- **We see:** the Ashes film in the story player. The Leader of the Van shouts "Get down on your knees!" in his own voice. At the turning point the film stops and asks "What will happen when the daimio passes by?" with two choices. The reader picks "Climb down and hide quietly", and the film begins to show that choice ("Imagine if the old man climbed down to kneel quietly...").
- **Narrator:** "Stories come alive too, with a voice for every character." / "At the turning point, you choose what happens."
- **Your phone (P3, optional, 6 seconds):** the same moment on the phone: tap one of the two choices.

### 8. Talk to a character (1:42 to 1:58)

- **Wow:** a character from the book talks back, in its own voice.
- **We see:** "Talk to Dog-Spirit" opens. The reader asks "Dog-Spirit, why did you help the old man?" and the Dog-Spirit answers in character: "I helped him because he was always so kind and loving to me, and I wanted to bring joy back to his sweet heart."
- **Narrator:** "Then talk to the characters. They answer in their own voice."
- **Your phone (P3, optional, 8 seconds):** tap "Talk to Dog-Spirit" and ask your own question out loud.

### 9. Made, and checked, by Google AI (1:58 to 2:18)

- **Wow:** an AI critic watches every film and sends weak shots back.
- **We see:** a drawn diagram that builds up with the narration, using the real material of the Ashes film: the page, the plan, the cast sheet and a drawn frame, a filmed shot playing, the voices, and a real note from the reviewer's first round ("The palanquin glides by itself with no bearers carrying the poles." Shot 5: film it again), with an arrow back to "weak shots made again".
- **Narrator:** "Every film is made by Google AI." / "Gemini plans it, Nano Banana draws it in the book's own style, Gemini Omni films it, and Gemini gives every character a voice." / "Then Gemini watches the film like a critic, and sends weak shots back to be made again."

### 10. The same film for everyone (2:18 to 2:28)

- **Wow:** a second phone gets the same film in seconds, from another page of the story.
- **We see:** two phones scan two pages of the same story. Both play the same film after about 3 seconds.
- **Narrator:** "Every page is remembered." / "The next student who scans it, or any other page of the story, gets the same film in seconds."
- **Your phone (P4, 8 seconds):** two phones side by side, one over PDF page 3 and one over PDF page 4 (or page 21, a page that was never used to make the film). Tap both at about the same time.

### 11. For classrooms and other apps (2:28 to 2:39)

- **Wow:** one platform for students, teachers and other apps.
- **We see:** a quick montage: the quiz, the progress page, a chapter film, and a school's website with the lesson inside it.
- **Narrator:** "Students get quizzes and progress. Teachers get chapter films for the class." / "Other learning apps can add it all with one line of code."

### 12. End card (2:39 to 2:47)

- **We see:** "Any page, alive", the QR code to the app, and the Google technology used.
- **Narrator:** "Living Textbook. Any page, alive." / "Built with Gemini on Google Cloud."

## The files

All files are in `eval/output/demo-video-2`, which git ignores.

- `living-textbook-demo-placeholders.mp4`: the reference. Red notes mark where your phone recordings go.
- `living-textbook-demo.mp4`: the same video without the notes. You can submit it as it is.
- `living-textbook-demo-no-narrator.mp4`: the captions, the music and the app's own voices, without the narrator, for your own voice over.
- `narration.wav` and `captions.srt`: the narrator alone, and the captions with their times.

To put your recordings in, place each clip over its placeholder: P1 at 0:00 to 0:22, P2 at 0:44 to 0:49, P3 (optional) at 1:22 to 1:58, and P4 at 2:18 to 2:28. `timeline.json` has the exact times of every shot, and `captions.srt` the times of every line. Keep the app's own voices (the story, the tutor, the Dog-Spirit) from the reference or from your phone's screen recording. The `-small` copies are under 30 MB, for a phone.

## Facts the narration uses

- "In about 20 seconds": a lesson is ready in 14 to 20 seconds (measured on Cloud Run).
- "In seconds": a known story page finds its film in 2.9 to 3.1 seconds on Cloud Run (measured on 2026-10-18).
- The models: Gemini 4 Argon plans and reviews the films, Nano Banana 2.1 draws, Gemini Omni Flash films, Gemini 3.8 TTS speaks, Lyria writes music, Gemini Live is the tutor and the characters, Gemini Embedding 2 remembers the pages, Gemini Flash builds the lessons.
